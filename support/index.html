Support

Write to support@cennaa.app. Say what you were doing and what you expected; a screenshot helps.

Quick answers

A post I shared says it needs the recipe text.
Neither the caption nor the audio held a full recipe. Paste it from the comments in the sheet, or later from the Recipes tab.
Draft this week says it needs Cenna Plus.
The free plan drafts the current week once. Drafting other weeks, redrafting, and re-rolls are part of Cenna Plus, which starts with a 7-day free trial.
The list has an amount that looks wrong.
Amounts come from your recipes, merged and rounded up to what shops sell. Tap the line to change it, or tell us: a wrong unit is something we fix for everyone.
How do I cancel Cenna Plus?
Settings on your iPhone, your name, Subscriptions, Cenna. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
How do I delete my account?
Cenna, Settings, Delete account. Everything you saved goes with it, straight away.
Can I get my data out?
Cenna, Settings, Export data. You get a file with your recipes, plans and lists.
