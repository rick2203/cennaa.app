What Cenna is

Cenna saves recipes you find, plans your week from them, writes a grocery list, reads photos of your kitchen, and walks you through cooking. Recipes, plans and lists are produced in part by automated systems and can be wrong: an amount misread from a video, a step out of order, a macro estimate off. Check anything that matters, especially allergens, and cook safely.
